🐘 Stock Market Elephant/ Scaling Playbook

The prop-firm scaling playbook

The multi-firm sequencing plan: start on $50K evaluations, pass one, and let each payout fund the next account — never fresh capital after the first. One tested edge, executed once, copied everywhere.

The architecture: one master, copy everywhere

Prove your edge on one master account first. Once it survives live, a trade copier (e.g. PickMyTrade) mirrors every fill to accounts at other firms — one decision, many payouts. Payouts recycle into more accounts.

THE CATCH
A copier multiplies mistakes at the same speed as profits, and every firm's drawdown rules differ (EOD-trailing vs intraday, daily-loss policies). Run each firm's ruleset through the EV calculator before its account goes live.

0 Start with zero (or near-zero) capital

Free-account giveaways exist in trading communities; if buying, never pay list price — discount codes are everywhere. Stick to $50K evaluation accounts: the drawdown-to- target ratios are the friendliest in the space and the whole playbook is built around them.

1 First firm: Tradeify Growth

Straightforward rules, flexible structure, and a $500 minimum withdrawal — the fastest route to a first real payout.

ADVANCE WHEN
Eval passed and first $500 payout secured.

2 Recycle into Lucid Trading

The first payout funds a Lucid Flex or Pro account. Goal: minimum payouts from both firms simultaneously — the start of compounding.

ADVANCE WHEN
Concurrent payouts from both; profits recycled into more accounts — target 4–5 funded accounts running at once.

3 Add My Funded Futures Pro

Once several accounts pay consistently, MFF Pro's $100K SIM cap allows the "home run" holds the smaller accounts can't carry.

ADVANCE WHEN
$400–500/day consistent across max-allocated accounts.

4 Veteran layer: Take Profit Trader

Excellent daily payout structure once you've built a buffer — the last piece of the four-firm stack.

Risk rules (non-negotiable)

  • Risk per trade = max drawdown ÷ 10. On a $50K account with a $2,000 drawdown, that's $200 per trade — ten straight losers must not kill an account.
  • NASDAQ translation: 2–5 micro contracts with a 20–50 point stop.
  • One payout at a time: hit the minimum, withdraw it, fund the next account. Compound accounts, not position size — sizing up raises bust risk as fast as reward.
