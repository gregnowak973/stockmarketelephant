The numbers, in pictures

Sep 9, 2026 · charts, minimal words

Words can hide things; pictures can't. Here is our research program in six charts — real CME MNQ data, ~1,900 sessions, realistic costs, all reproducible from the methods described across this series.

1 · Seven years of equity, one micro contract

Thin, real, unglamorous: ~$15k over 7.3 years at minimum size. The shape matters more than the total — persistent grind, no hockey stick.

2 · Drawdown from peak — the part sales pages crop out

Worst excursion ~$4,600, and — honesty compels — the deepest one is the most recent. A drawdown chart is a strategy's true biography.

3 · Year by year — the edge is not evenly spread

Two flat years, five good ones, a soft current year. A backtest where every year is green is a backtest you should question.

4 · The win-rate trap, visualized

Total profit by profit-target. The 0.5R version wins 66% of trades and makes a third of the money. Everything from 1R up is statistically identical (we tested).

6 · The exam that matters: out-of-sample paths

Five full CPCV out-of-sample reconstructions of history (annualized Sharpe each). All positive, tightly clustered — honestly-chosen parameters kept working on data they never saw, 15 splits out of 15.

None of this proves the future. It proves we didn't fool ourselves about the past — the most a backtest can honestly claim, and more than most ever establish.
